Not Me But You Walkathon ’26 Narayana MMI Hospital Heart Health & Fitness Community Wellness Step For Health Active Living

27 Sep 2026 · Health & Community Wellness

Every step counts, every stride for health.

NSS IIIT Naya Raipur is pleased to share that Walkathon ’26, organized by Narayana MMI Hospital, Raipur, was enthusiastically held on Sunday, 27 September 2026 to promote cardiovascular health, active living, and community wellness.

Led by the NSS Unit and volunteer contingent, participants gathered early morning for the flag-off alongside medical professionals and citizens from across Raipur. Taking strides through the city with awareness banners and energetic slogans, the walkathon inspired people to embrace walking as a daily healthy habit. The event witnessed spirited participation, fostering community camaraderie and emphasizing preventive healthcare.
