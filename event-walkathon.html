27 Sep 2026 · Community Wellness

Every step matters, every heart beats stronger.

NSS IIIT Naya Raipur is pleased to share that Walkathon ’26, organized by Narayana MMI Hospital, Raipur, was enthusiastically held on Sunday, 27 September 2026 to promote heart health and active lifestyles.

Guided by NSS Program Officer Dr. Krishnanand Vishwakarma, students, faculty, healthcare experts, and city residents gathered in high spirits at the morning flag-off. Carrying motivational banners and awareness placards, the contingent covered a vibrant route across Raipur, inspiring bystanders to incorporate walking into their everyday routines. The initiative highlighted preventive healthcare and celebrated the power of community walking in maintaining cardiovascular vitality.
