About the unit

Rooted in the campus, working beyond it.

NSS IIIT Naya Raipur is the student volunteer wing that turns the scheme's national motto into weekly, on-ground work — from plantation drives on campus to outreach in the villages around Naya Raipur.

Every volunteer signs up for the same reason: to spend a few hours a week on something bigger than a syllabus. Programme officers guide the unit, but events, outreach and design — this website included — are run by students.
