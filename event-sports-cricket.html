15 Sep 2026 · Athletic Excellence

Passion on the pitch, unity in every over.

In commemoration of National Sports Day, celebrated across India in honour of hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand, the NSS Unit and Sports Committee of IIIT Naya Raipur organized an exciting campus cricket tournament on 15th September 2026.

Conducted under the guidance of NSS Program Officer Dr. Krishnanand Vishwakarma, the event promoted physical health, team synergy, and competitive discipline under the Khelo India and Fit India initiatives. Teams from undergraduate programs, postgraduate batches, and faculty members clashed in thrilling matches marked by spectacular fielding, strategic bowling spells, and boundary celebrations. The tournament fostered lasting bonds across branches and concluded with a grand trophy presentation.
